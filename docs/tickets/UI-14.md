# UI-14

Authoritative state: [master ticket](../master-tickets.md#ui-14).

## User story

As an editor, I want each timeline region to read as one card with its name in a dark top band and
one source still at its head, so I can tell clips apart without a repeating filmstrip.

## Owner decision

Mite picked option B from the
[options A-E](https://ampcode.com/artifacts/visuals/clip-region-card-options-a-e-502496bd-bcc3-469a-95ee-c6737ace7890):
"i like b. the clip name needs to go in that darker band at the top" and "B but the clip name needs
to come up so its ceneted within the darker band at the top of the clip". Build from the
[B prototype](https://ampcode.com/artifacts/visuals/clip-region-card-b-prototype-ui-sdk-9eb779a0-0ec4-4af9-af1b-a0abaac6d454)
by design owner [T-e2082a9f](https://ampcode.com/threads/T-e2082a9f-5cd0-43fc-8960-e0c6f69f8a86).

Clipper already depends on Vercel's UI SDK (`@ui-sdk/core` and `@ui-sdk/react` 0.1.0-beta.5, added
in `eb55a36` for icon button tooltips). Mite then directed the design owner: "any opportunity we
have to implement ui sdk to improve the feeling of a UI interaction should be explored. do a full
migrattion in the branch and test it. show me irrefutable proof that no regressions happened or bugs
were introduced." UI-14 builds on that migration's span and selection primitives once its candidate
is accepted, or on the existing gestures if not.

## Acceptance

1. Region: 47 px tall, 1 px border, 4 px radius, track colour tokens. A 14 px dark band (`#00000040`
   over the track colour) spans the top; the name is 9 px, weight 500, ellipsized and vertically
   centred in it. The duration sits right in 8 px IBM Plex Mono at 65% opacity and shrinks away
   before the name does. The band stays dark in every state.
2. Video regions show one still under the band: 32 px tall, 16:9 (56.9 x 32), `object-fit: cover`,
   left-anchored, its right rule a box-shadow. Its size never follows region width; narrow regions
   crop it. It shows the source frame at the region's in-point, so a start trim swaps the frame at
   the same size. The rest of the body is solid track colour. This replaces the tiled `.filmstrip`
   and closes the "Filmstrips repeat the first thumbnail" note.
3. Audio regions have no still. The waveform sits under the band (top 16 px, 28 px tall) at fixed
   source-time detail offset by the in-point, cropped, never scaled. Coordinate this hunk with
   UI-13, which owns waveform data.
4. Hover lifts the body colour and shows the edge grips. Selected and focus-visible change colour
   only (REF-59): no change to any region's x, y, width or height, which a test asserts.
5. The head still stays 56.9 x 32 through real end-trim, start-trim and body-move drags and keyboard
   moves on video and audio regions. Inspect rendered captures at fit and deep zoom, at 1440 and 390
   px, with long and very short regions.
6. Keep existing trim, drag, Razor, lock and Delete behaviour, Save/Open, and responsive playback.
   Usual exact-candidate review, serialized delivery and exact-SHA green CI.

## Boundaries

Likely paths: `src/Timeline.tsx` region markup, `src/styles.css` region rules, the thumbnail source
in `src/media.ts` if a still per in-point needs it, `src/Timeline.stories.tsx` and one spec. Do not
take UI-8's library-row thumbnails or UI-13's waveform data. REF-58 adds zoom-region edges in the
zoom lane, not clip regions.
