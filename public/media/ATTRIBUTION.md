## music.wav

Original synthesized C3/G3/C4 ambient chord, generated for this demo with FFmpeg. Dedicated to the
public domain under CC0 1.0 by the project contributor. No third-party music recording was used.

The 14-second mono 44.1 kHz signal is
`0.08*sin(2*PI*130.81*t)+0.05*sin(2*PI*196*t)+0.03*sin(2*PI*261.63*t)`. It fades in for one second
and fades out from 12 to 14 seconds.
